About this Template
A SaaS startup template with 6 ready-made Tailwind CSS pages for marketing, pricing, customer credibility, and company storytelling. It is designed for founders, product marketers, and teams that need a polished website for launching and growing software products.

Features
Landing page foundation – Present product value with a modern startup-focused layout.
Features and pricing pages – Explain capabilities, packaging, and value in dedicated conversion-ready views.
Customer proof pages – Support trust-building with customer listings and customer detail storytelling.
About page – Add company background, brand context, and team positioning.
Responsive marketing layout – Sections, cards, and content adapt across desktop and smaller screens.
Light and dark mode – Supports modern product-site styling.
Tailwind CSS customization – Adjust hero sections, pricing, testimonials, and navigation to match your brand.
Documentation and updates – Includes guidance for setup, customization, and ongoing improvements.
