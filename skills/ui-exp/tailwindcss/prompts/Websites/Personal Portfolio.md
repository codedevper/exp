About this Template
A personal portfolio template with 3 ready-made Tailwind CSS pages for profile storytelling, selected work, and social proof. It is designed for independent professionals and creators who need a minimal website for presenting themselves with clarity and polish.

Features
Editorial profile page – Present your background, identity, and positioning from one polished landing page.
Selected work page – Showcase projects, case studies, and creative output in a dedicated portfolio view.
Reviews page – Add testimonials and social proof to support credibility.
Minimal content structure – Good foundation for personal brands, freelance businesses, and creator portfolios.
Responsive website layout – Typography, content blocks, and images adapt across desktop and smaller screens.
Light and dark mode – Supports modern personal-site styling.
Tailwind CSS customization – Adjust typography, editorial rhythm, work sections, and testimonials to match your brand.
Documentation and updates – Includes guidance for setup, customization, and ongoing improvements.
