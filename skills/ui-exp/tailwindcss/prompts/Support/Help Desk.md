About this Template
A help desk template with 5 ready-made Tailwind CSS pages for chat-based support, visitor tracking, tickets, activity, and reports. It is designed for customer service products and internal support teams that need a structured workspace for handling conversations and service operations.

Features
Support workspace – Manage conversations and user context in a focused service interface.
Visitors page – Review visitor activity and support touchpoints from a dedicated view.
Tickets and activity pages – Extend service workflows across triage, follow-up, and status tracking.
Reports page – Present support performance and team metrics in a polished dashboard-style layout.
Conversation-first patterns – Good foundation for chat panels, tags, filters, and service states.
Responsive application layout – Panels, tables, and messaging UI adapt across screen sizes.
Light and dark mode – Supports modern service product styling.
Tailwind CSS customization – Adjust navigation, conversations, tables, and reporting views to fit your process.
