About this Template
A marketplace template with 4 ready-made Tailwind CSS pages for discovery, category browsing, listings, and product details. It is designed for multi-vendor and discovery-heavy shopping experiences that need clean navigation and strong product exploration patterns.

Features
Marketplace landing page – Present discovery and navigation from a clean front-door experience.
Categories and listing coverage – Organize browsing across vendor and catalog exploration flows.
Product detail page – Showcase item-specific content from a dedicated commerce detail view.
Discovery-first structure – A useful base for search, filtering, and multi-vendor browsing patterns.
Responsive ecommerce layout – Cards, menus, and product modules adapt across screen sizes.
Light and dark mode – Supports modern marketplace styling.
Tailwind CSS customization – Adjust discovery views, navigation, cards, and product screens to match your brand.
Documentation and updates – Includes guidance for setup, customization, and ongoing improvements.
