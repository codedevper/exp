About this Template
A CMS admin template with 6 ready-made Tailwind CSS pages for editorial dashboards, content operations, member management, post creation, drafts, and published content views. It is designed for content teams that need a clean workflow for planning, editing, reviewing, and publishing.

Features
Editorial overview dashboard – Monitor content activity, featured posts, author highlights, and publishing momentum from one screen.
Posts management – Review article lists, scan statuses, and manage content in an interface tailored for editorial workflows.
Members page – Organize contributors, editors, and internal collaborators in a clean management view.
Create post flow – Start drafting new content quickly with a ready-made page for composing and structuring articles.
Draft and published views – Separate in-progress and live content states for clearer publishing operations.
Responsive application layout – Sidebar, header, cards, and content panels adapt across desktop and smaller screens.
Light and dark mode – Supports modern product styling for editorial tools used throughout the day.
Tailwind CSS customization – Adjust navigation, panels, cards, forms, and lists to match your content brand and workflow.
Publishing-focused UI patterns – Includes layouts that fit blogs, media products, newsletters, and internal content hubs.
Workflow-ready structure – Good foundation for extending approvals, scheduling, analytics, and team collaboration flows.
Documentation – Step-by-step guidance for setup, customization, and usage.
12 months of updates – Keep the template current with ongoing improvements.
